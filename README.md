# 🎶 Sines  
**Virtual Analog Synthesizer by Cherry Audio**

**Sines** by **Cherry Audio** is a modern polyphonic synthesizer inspired by the simplicity and beauty of sine waves.  
It delivers warm, evolving tones and smooth modulation that recall vintage analog hardware while offering deep modern control.

## ✨ Features
- **Four Oscillators** – based on pure sine waves with morphing to complex shapes  
- **Wave Morphing and Phase Modulation** – for dynamic harmonic motion  
- **Dual Filters and LFOs** – shape sound with precision and warmth  
- **Built-in FX Rack** – reverb, delay, chorus, and phaser  
- **Modern Polyphony** – up to 32 voices with unison and glide  
- **Formats:** VST3 / AU / AAX / Standalone (Windows & macOS)

## 🧠 Why Sines
Sines offers an elegant balance between minimalism and creativity.  
It’s perfect for pads, evolving textures, and organic tones — a great choice for ambient, chillout, and cinematic sound design.

## 💻 Requirements
- Windows 10+ / macOS 10.13+  
- 64-bit DAW supporting VST3, AU, or AAX  
- 4 GB RAM minimum
